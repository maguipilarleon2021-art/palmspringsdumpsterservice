Frequently Asked Questions

1. What size dumpster do I need for my project in #State?

The right size depends on the type and volume of debris. Small cleanouts and minor renovations typically require a 10-yard dumpster. Medium remodeling or roofing jobs often need a 20-yard container. Larger construction or demolition projects may require 30- or 40-yard dumpsters. Our team helps you choose based on your project scope.

2. How long can I keep a dumpster rental in #State?

Rental periods vary depending on your project needs. Standard rentals typically range from several days to a week, but extensions are available. If your project timeline changes, we can adjust your pickup schedule accordingly.

3. What materials are allowed in a dumpster?

Most household debris, construction materials, furniture, drywall, wood, shingles, yard waste, and general junk are accepted. Hazardous materials such as chemicals, paint, tires, batteries, and certain electronics may require special disposal and are typically not allowed. Always confirm before loading restricted items.

4. Do I need a permit for dumpster rental in #State?

If the dumpster is placed on private property such as a driveway, permits are usually not required. However, if placement is on a public street or sidewalk, local regulations in #State may require a permit. We can guide you through the process if needed.

5. What is the difference between roll-off, front-load, and rear-load dumpsters?

Roll-off dumpsters are open-top containers delivered for temporary projects like renovations and construction.Front-load dumpsters are used for scheduled commercial waste collection and are emptied by specialized trucks. Rear-load dumpsters are compact containers ideal for properties with limited space but consistent waste needs.
